# Writing the first message

The message's job is to earn a reply to a small ask, not to explain the product.

## Structure (email, ≤ profile word limit)

1. **Observation:** one specific, verified fact about them, ideally the "neden şimdi" signal. `[cNNN]`
2. **Why it matters:** one sentence connecting that fact to the problem in the profile. Say it as a question or a common pattern, not a diagnosis of their business ("şube sayısı arttıkça ... zorlaşıyor mu?").
3. **What we do:** at most one sentence, only verified product facts. `[kNNN]`
4. **Ask:** one small, concrete request (e.g. a 15-minute call or a pilot conversation). No meeting-link pressure.
5. Signature from `[sender]`. Mention `[sender].context` if the pack says it may be used.

Subject: short, specific to them, no clickbait. Example: `Şube sayısı ve günlük sipariş`.

LinkedIn: same logic in ≤ the character limit. Observation, question, ask. No product pitch.

## Hard rules

- A marker after every factual sentence: `[c001]` for lead evidence, `[k001]` for product knowledge. Sentences without markers must contain no facts (greeting, question, ask).
- No numbers without a cited source, except the pack's `allowed_numbers`.
- If `[product].stage` is `pilot`, promise no results: no percentages, no ROI, no "X% azaltır".
- Do not state their internal problems as fact. "İsrafınız çok" is a guess; "yorumlarda akşam ürünlerin tükendiği yazıyor [c004]" is evidence.
- No flattery you cannot back up ("sektörün lideri", "harika işler").
- Never cite a complaint that embarrasses the recipient (hygiene, food safety, health, staff behaviour, legal disputes), even if it is verified. A complaint may be mentioned only when it is about the product's problem, and then as a pattern ("yorumlarda akşam ürünlerin tükendiği yazıyor"), never by quoting or naming the complainant.
- No fake familiarity ("geçen hafta konuşmuştuk"), no false urgency, no fake reply threads (`Re:`).
- Address the decision maker by name only if a verified `person_title` exists; otherwise use a neutral greeting.

## Avoid AI-sounding writing

No "Umarım bu e-posta sizi iyi bulur", no "hızla değişen dünyada", no "sadece X değil, aynı zamanda Y",
no three-adjective lists, no em-dash chains, no "devrim niteliğinde / oyun değiştirici". Write like
a person who looked at their business for ten minutes and has one honest question.
